Profile

Research-oriented work across urban economics, family economics, housing, and applied microeconomics, with a focus on turning complex data into transparent and reproducible evidence.

Research experience

  • Research assistance. Support empirical projects through literature review, data construction and validation, spatial analysis, document synthesis, and research-facing tools.
  • Urban Evidence Observatory. Develop a private literature workflow that organizes metadata, abstracts, methods and data signals, and citation relationships for urban-economics research.
  • Housing and urban upgrading. Build a multi-year satellite-imagery pipeline for Kenyan informal settlements, including settlement boundaries, image retrieval, mosaicking, cropping, and cross-temporal sampling.

Methods and tools

Applied microeconomics · Data construction · GIS and remote sensing · Python · SQL · Reproducible research workflows
