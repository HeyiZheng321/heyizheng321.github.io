Heyi Zheng

economics · cities · data

I am Heyi Zheng. I work on economic questions at the intersection of households, cities, and data.

My interests span urban economics, family economics, housing, and applied microeconomics. I build reproducible workflows that make difficult data—especially geospatial and literature data—usable for research.

Research · CV · Blog · GitHub

Current work

  • Urban evidence

    A private, analysis-first literature observatory that connects paper metadata, abstracts, methods and data signals, and citation networks to support research discovery.

  • Housing and urban upgrading

    A multi-year satellite-imagery workflow for informal settlements in Kenya, with attention to housing investment, roof materials, and the limits of visual measurement.

  • Research assistance and infrastructure

    Data construction and validation, spatial processing, literature synthesis, reproducible scripts, and research-facing dashboards that help empirical projects move from raw material to evidence.
